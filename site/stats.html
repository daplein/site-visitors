        How this works

        Each visitor is counted once per month, and once overall. To tell visitors apart, the counter keeps a scrambled code (a salted hash) made from your IP address and browser. Your IP address itself is never stored.

        Cloudflare works out your country, region and city from the connection, and your browser tells whether you're on a phone or a computer. That's all that's kept: no cookies and no tracking across sites. Cities only show here once at least 3 people have visited from them.

        The code is open: github.com/daplein/site-visitors
